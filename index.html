FOR TEACHERS & FAMILIES

A little support goes a long way.

Each course includes weekly learning goals, activities, and teaching guidance. Pick the course, open its guide, and learn alongside your maker.

Scratch Coding guideArduino teacher guideScratch Games handbook

Learning from home? Use a current browser with a mouse or touch screen. Download work regularly; progress in the labs stays on that browser. Ask your teacher where to submit assignments.

Building with Arduino? The 3D models explain ideas. Physical builds need an approved kit, real hardware tests, and adult supervision. The browser does not run arbitrary Arduino code.
